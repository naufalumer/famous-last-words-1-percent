The funniest last words a phone says when its battery hits 1 percent, in a single line of 97 characters. The phone was never dying on schedule: it lingered at 1 percent for twenty minutes, running one last experiment on its owner.

> I have been at 1 percent for twenty minutes. I stayed alive just to see who you would call first.

## Why this line

The judge brief bans the stock moves of the genre — the will reading, the goodbye to the charger, the pun on 1 percent — so the line dodges all three. Instead it reframes the dying moment as something the phone chose: it stayed alive to see who its owner would call first. The battery is the subject, the voice is the phone's own, the punch lands in the last words, and it reads on the first pass with nothing to explain. Alternates considered (a survivor's swagger list, a search-history reversal, a blame-the-owner cut-off) are noted in the work notes; this one won because its twist is the biggest surprise and its final beat turns the owner's panic back on them.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (`/output` by default). Standard library only, no input, no network.
